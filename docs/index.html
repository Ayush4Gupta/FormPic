Frequently Asked Questions

Why do SSC, UPSC, and IBPS reject photos larger than 50 KB?

Government examination portals (like SSC CGL/CHSL, UPSC CSE, and IBPS Bank PO) process tens of millions of candidate applications within tight deadlines. To prevent server crashes and maintain fast loading, their automated upload engines enforce strict file-size limits (typically strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB). If an image is 50.1 KB, the upload gateway automatically rejects it. ValidPic uses binary search compression to ensure your final image is strictly under the required threshold while preserving maximum facial sharpness.

Are my sensitive ID photos uploaded to any server?

Never. 100% of processing occurs directly inside your browser. Unlike other online compressors that send your photos to remote cloud servers, ValidPic executes entirely via local HTML5 Canvas APIs on your phone or computer. Zero bytes of your photos are ever uploaded or stored anywhere.

How do I resize my signature to under 20 KB?

Click on the "Official Signature" tab or tap the Under 20 KB chip. Upload a photo or scan of your signature on white paper. ValidPic automatically enhances the ink contrast, eliminates paper shadows, and compresses the signature into an application-compliant JPEG between 10 KB and 20 KB.

Does ValidPic support iPhone photos and mobile screenshots?

Yes. ValidPic seamlessly converts images taken with iPhones (HEIC), Android cameras, digital cameras, scanners, and mobile screenshots into standard, exam-ready .jpg files.
