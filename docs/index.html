Frequently Asked Questions

Why do SSC, UPSC, and IBPS reject photos larger than 50 KB?

Government examination servers receive millions of applications within short deadlines. To maintain server uptime, their portals enforce hard file-size limits (typically strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB). If an uploaded image is even 50.1 KB, the upload parser automatically rejects it. ValidPic's binary search compression engine guarantees your file remains strictly under the required threshold.

Are my personal photos uploaded to any server?

Never. 100% of image processing runs client-side in your web browser. Unlike other online resizers that upload your sensitive ID photos to remote cloud servers, ValidPic uses HTML5 Canvas APIs on your computer or phone CPU. Zero bytes are uploaded to the internet.

How do I compress my signature to under 20 KB?

Select the "Official Signature" preset from the list or tap the 20 KB quick button. Upload a photo or scan of your signature on white paper. ValidPic enhances the black/blue ink contrast and compresses the image to between 10 KB and 20 KB in compliant JPEG format.

Does ValidPic support iPhone photos and screenshots?

Yes. ValidPic accepts photos clicked on iPhones, Android phones, digital cameras, flatbed scanners, and mobile screenshots (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC). It automatically formats and exports a standard, application-ready .jpg file.
