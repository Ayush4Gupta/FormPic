## Step 1: Create a Free Samsung Developer Account ($0)

1. Open the **[Samsung Galaxy Store Developer Portal](https://seller.samsungapps.com/)** in your web browser.
2. Click **"Sign In"** or **"Join Now"**:
   - If you already have a Samsung account (used on any Samsung phone or TV), you can log in with it.
   - Otherwise, click **"Create account"** (free).
3. Select Account Type:
   - Choose **"Private Developer"** (Individual).
4. Fill in your basic information:
   - **Developer Name**: Enter your name (e.g. *Ayush Gupta*) or brand name (*ValidPic*).
   - **Email & Phone Number**: For verification codes.
   - **Country**: India (or your country of residence).
5. Accept the **Samsung Galaxy Store Developer Agreement** and submit.
   > [!NOTE]
   > Samsung developer registration is completely free. No credit card or registration fee is required.

## Step 2: Add a New Application

1. In the Samsung Seller Portal dashboard, click **"Add New Application"** (top right button).
2. Select **"Android"**.
3. Set Default Language: **"English (United States)"** or **"English (India)"**.
4. Click **"Save"**.

## Step 4: Upload Your Binary (`app-release.apk`)

1. Click on the **"Binary"** or **"App Files"** tab.
2. Click **"Add Binary"**.
3. Choose **"Google Play / Android APK"**.
4. Select and upload your downloaded [`app-release.apk`](https://github.com/Ayush4Gupta/ValidPic/releases/download/v1.0.8/app-release.apk).
5. Samsung will automatically parse the file and detect:
   * **Package Name**: `com.validpic.app`
   * **Version Code**: `9`
   * **Version Name**: `1.0.8`
   * **Min SDK**: `24` (Android 7.0+)
   * **Target SDK**: `34` (Android 14)
6. Click **"Save"**.

1. Verify that all sections on the left sidebar have a green checkmark (`✓`).
2. Click the blue **"Submit"** button at the top right.
3. **Samsung Review Timeline**:
   * Samsung's QA team typically reviews apps in **2 to 4 business days**.
   * You will receive an email confirmation as soon as ValidPic is approved and published on the Samsung Galaxy Store!

## Step 7: Link Samsung Galaxy Store in Google AdMob (Lifting Ad Limits!)

Once your app is approved on Samsung Galaxy Store:

1. Get your public Samsung store URL (format: `https://galaxystore.samsung.com/detail/com.validpic.app`).
2. Open your AdMob dashboard:
   👉 **[AdMob App Store Link Settings](https://admob.google.com/v2/apps/6432499174/update?ctp=%2Fapps%2F6432499174%2Foverview&appPlatform=2&onboardingTaskStep=FIRST_APP_LINK)**
3. Under **Supported app stores**, select **"Samsung Galaxy Store"**.
4. Paste your Samsung Galaxy Store URL or enter `com.validpic.app`.
5. Click **"Save"**.
6. Google AdMob will verify the link within 24–48 hours, switch your status from **"Requires review"** to **"Ready"**, and **enable live ad serving on all devices globally!**
