# FormPic — Google Play Store Publishing & ASO Strategy

## 1. App Store Optimization (ASO) Metadata

### App Title (30 characters max)
`FormPic: Passport & Exam Photo` (30 characters)

### Short Description (80 characters max)
`Passport & exam photos with clean white background. Exact Under 50 KB guarantee.` (80 characters)

### Full Description (Up to 4000 characters)
```markdown
Need an application-ready passport or exam photo in seconds?

Most Indian government portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NEET, Railway, State PSCs) reject photos if they are even 1 KB over the limit or lack a plain white background. 

FormPic solves this instantly. Simply take a selfie or pick a photo from your gallery. FormPic automatically aligns your face, replaces messy backgrounds with pure clean white, and compresses your image strictly under your required file size.

⭐ WHY CHOOSE FORMPIC?

✅ EXACT FILE SIZE GUARANTEE (UNDER 25 KB / 50 KB / 75 KB / 100 KB)
Never face "File size exceeds 50 KB" rejection again. Our intelligent engine verifies the exact byte count so your photo is guaranteed to upload smoothly.

✅ 100% ON-DEVICE PRIVACY
Your photos NEVER leave your phone. All face detection, background isolation, and compression run locally on your device using advanced on-device AI. Zero cloud uploads.

✅ CLEAN WHITE BACKGROUND
Our on-device segmentation tool cleanly isolates your hair and shoulders, replacing cluttered or uneven backgrounds with compliant pure white.

✅ OFFICIAL INDIAN APPLICATION PRESETS
Pre-calibrated according to official published guidelines:
• Indian Passport (Passport Seva / MEA): 35 × 45 mm, pure white background
• SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD): 20 KB – 50 KB, 3.5 × 4.5 cm
• UPSC (Civil Services, NDA, CDS): 20 KB – 300 KB, 3/4th face coverage
• IBPS & SBI (Bank PO & Clerk): 200 × 230 px, Under 50 KB
• NTA NEET UG & CUET: 10 KB – 200 KB, 80% face coverage
• Railway RRB (NTPC, Group D, ALP): Under 50 KB
• PAN Card (NSDL / UTIITSL): Under 50 KB
• Custom Size: Enter your custom KB and pixel requirements anytime.

✅ BUILT-IN PASSPORT CAMERA GUIDE
Our live viewfinder overlay helps you position your head, eyes, and chin at the exact official proportions before taking the shot.

✅ BEFORE / AFTER COMPARISON
Inspect your clean white background and official crop before downloading.

✅ NO COMPLICATED MENUS
No need to understand DPI, aspect ratios, or JPEG quality algorithms. Just select your target size and tap Download!

---

💡 PRIVACY GUARANTEE:
FormPic does not collect, store, or upload personal photos. Everything is processed offline on your smartphone.

*Disclaimer: FormPic prepares photos strictly according to published portal guidelines. FormPic is an independent utility and is not affiliated with any government agency.*
```
